How it works

Write it up. It explains itself. They approve.

01 · INTAKE

Snap the door jamb. The RO fills itself.

Your tech photographs the car. RO Engine reads the VIN sticker, the odometer and the plate, and builds the record — with timestamped photos of the car's condition before you touch it.

02 · RECOMMEND

The AI writes the maintenance on every car.

Your best advisor is two hours into a cylinder head job. The 2001 Corolla in for an oil change still gets the full write-up: the factory schedule, the car's own history and what's actually due. Normal or severe service, your call.

  • Overdue and coming-due work surfaced automatically
  • Deferred work remembered from past visits
  • Plain-language reasons the customer understands
03 · APPROVE

The estimate explains it. They approve it from the couch.

Every service says what it is, why it's due now and what happens if they wait. A private link — no app, no login. Line by line, approve or decline. The answer lands on the RO, timestamped and in writing, and declines become next visit's reminders.
