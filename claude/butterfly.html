This is the Lorenz system: three small equations, originally a stripped-down model of air rising and falling, that never settle and never repeat. Two copies run side by side, amber and blue, started almost exactly in the same place.

Each one plays the note under its left-right position, on a minor pentatonic scale. At first they are the same path and you hear one voice. The difference between them doubles roughly every three quarters of a second, so after half a minute or so a billionth has become the whole width of the shape, and they split into two melodies that never line up again.

The graph at the bottom shows the distance between them on a log scale. It climbs in a nearly straight line, which is what "exponential" looks like, until it hits the size of the attractor and can't grow any more. The dashed line is the rate the math predicts.
