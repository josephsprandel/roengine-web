Connect or reconnect QuickBooks

The connection is made from inside your shop's RO Engine, so it belongs to your shop

Each shop has its own RO Engine address, like yourshop.roengine.com. It's in your welcome email and it's the address you sign in at every day.

  1. Sign in to your shop's RO Engine as a user with the Accounting permission (by default, the shop's owners).
  2. Go to Settings → Accounting and click Connect to QuickBooks (or Reconnect QuickBooks if it shows).
  3. Sign in to Intuit with a QuickBooks login that is an admin of the company, pick the company and approve the connection. Intuit sends you back to RO Engine.
  4. Pick a start date and the accounts our entries post to. Entries for repair orders closed on or after the start date are sent from then on.

Reconnecting to the same QuickBooks company picks up where it left off: anything recorded while you were disconnected is sent, and nothing already sent is posted again.
