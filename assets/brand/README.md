the tagline's initials — **A**utomotive **I**ntelligence — spell A.I.

**Construction** (approved 2026-09-26, "A · refined, exponent 72%")
- `RO` — Archivo, weight 900, width 118%, letter-spacing −0.035em
- `e` — STIX Two Text Italic, weight 600, 72% of the RO size, 0.05em gap,
  raised 0.18em — a true math-italic exponent (STIX is the typeface of
  mathematical publishing), large enough to never read as a ™
- Outlines generated with fontTools from positions measured in Chrome, then
  verified by overlay against the live-font render (edge-only difference).

**Colors**
- Navy `#14263f` (RO on light) · Teal `#3fb5ae` (e on light)
- White `#ffffff` (RO on dark) · Bright teal `#4fd1c9` (e on dark)

**Tagline** "Automotive Intelligence" in small caps, wide tracking, with the
**A** and **I** in teal.

Keep clear space of at least the height of the `e` around the mark. At 16px the
exponent disappears — use the icon, not the mark, below ~24px tall.
