How it works

Keep Shop-Ware running until you're sure.

  1. Set up your shop. Start the free trial and you get your own RO Engine at yourshop.roengine.com. Nothing charges for 90 days.
  2. Give us a Shop-Ware login for the import. We use it to read your records and nothing else. Change the password when we're done.
  3. We run the import. It reads every repair order in full and loads it into your shop. Larger histories take longer; photos are what take the time.
  4. Check it side by side. Open a few repair orders in both systems. We check the totals match; you check it looks like your shop.
  5. Switch when you're ready. Run both as long as you like. Cancel Shop-Ware only once you're comfortable.
Before you cancel Shop-Ware, get your photos out. Photo links in Shop-Ware stop working when the account closes, and a CSV export doesn't include the pictures. Our import downloads every one, which is a good reason to do it before you cancel, not after.
The honest part
