RO Engine

Release notes

What changed in RO Engine, dated. Tell me what's broken and it shows up here. — Joe

Current release: 0.9.0 · build 3afbcb23 · November 1, 2026
Changes run at AutoHouse first. Anything tagged AutoHouse first reaches every shop with the next release.

November 1, 2026

  • The phone assistant can ring the desk about a waiting call-back. Turn on Call-back escalation in Settings → Phone Assistant: a call-back nobody has handled after 15 open minutes makes the desk phone ring, and the assistant says who called, about which car and why. Say you've got it and it's marked handled. If nobody answers it tries once more, then alerts Owners and Managers and texts your call-back list. Open hours only, off until you turn it on. AutoHouse first
  • See RO Engine the way a technician does. Click your name → View as Technician (or any role): the same buttons, prices hidden where they're hidden, read-only, with Exit at the top. Technicians no longer see send, close or void buttons they can't use. AutoHouse first
  • PAID only after a payment. A $0 repair order with nothing paid is no longer marked PAID. AutoHouse first
  • Customers pay by card from a text. Connect your shop's own Stripe account in Settings → Customer Payments, and every closed invoice's link gets a Pay button. Text payment link and Email payment link sit in the RO's Summary once every job is done and the invoice is closed. The customer pays from their phone, and the payment records itself on the RO. The money goes straight to your Stripe account; RO Engine takes no cut. Nothing payable shows until Stripe has finished verifying your shop. AutoHouse first
  • A receipt after every online payment. The customer gets an email with the paid invoice attached and, if you send automatic texts, a short text with a link to all their invoices. AutoHouse first
  • Money settings, easier to find. RO Engine Subscription is what you pay us (was Billing). Invoices & Tax holds RO numbering, tax and shop supplies (was Invoicing). Customer Payments puts your payment methods, the cash/check discount and online payments on one page. Old links still work. AutoHouse first

October 31, 2026

  • Check-in reads everything the photos show. The door label gives the VIN, build date and tire size. The plate gives the plate, the state, the registration sticker's month and the paint color. The vehicle card shows the registration date beside the plate, in red once it has passed, and you can set it in Edit Vehicle. AutoHouse first
  • Check-in photos show on the open RO right away, each labeled by what it shows: door jamb, odometer, plate, exterior. No reload. AutoHouse first
  • A car checked in from the phone is spelled "Toyota", not "TOYOTA". AutoHouse first
  • A calmer, roomier RO page. Service cards are shorter, with one settings row, a one-line description that grows as you type, and the service total on a plain line instead of a navy bar. Parts and labor get column heads once instead of labels on every row. Every amount uses the same font, and the cards are evenly spaced. A discount you haven't given shows as a grey $0.00 you can click, not a red one. Void invoice and Delete RO moved into the Actions menu. AutoHouse first

October 29, 2026

  • My day. Your day as blocks on an hour rail, from the tech app menu or the menu under your name. Jobs far over book are outlined in orange. Tap one, drag to how long it really took (or Keep book), tap where the rest went: another job on the same RO, Lunch, or something else. A lunch in the middle of a job comes out without touching either side. AutoHouse first
  • Settle your time when you finish a job. Marking a job complete or sending it to the advisor shows your clocked time against book on every labor line, already filled in. Usually it's one tap. A line done along with another one is recorded as 0 under it, so we learn which jobs overlap. AutoHouse first
  • Clocking out while you're still on a job asks first, with a way to fix the time. AutoHouse first
  • Job clock exceptions and a morning digest. Jobs far over book, techs clocked in with little job time, clocks left on overnight, finished work never settled. They show on Hours and in a 6 AM email to whoever runs the floor. You set the thresholds. AutoHouse first
  • One address box. Start typing and pick Google's suggestion: "202 N Redbud Ave, West Fork, AR 72774". City, state and ZIP still land where invoices and emails need them. AutoHouse first
  • Phone numbers format as you type, (479) 301-2880, everywhere you enter one. A pasted +1 number comes out right. AutoHouse first
  • Shorter Settings pages. Cards you set once fold to one line that shows what's set. Save buttons stay off until you've actually changed something. Opening a dropdown no longer nudges the page sideways. AutoHouse first
  • Decals and invoices show this visit's mileage, not the highest mileage the car ever had. One mistyped reading from an old visit no longer follows the car around. AutoHouse first

October 28, 2026

  • Payroll from the time clock. Each pay period adds up from everyone's clock-ins: regular, overtime and paid time off, by your shop's rules. Fix missed punches, finalize the period, and email it to your bookkeeper.
  • Every stage change is on record: who moved the car and when, from the RO, the job board, a transfer or check-in.
  • Ten more pages fit a phone.
  • "In stock" under a part only suggests a part with the same number. A same-name match is shown separately, to check it fits.

October 27, 2026

  • Parts Catalog type-ahead. Suggestions appear as you type.
  • Front or rear? Parts Catalog results show the axle, with a Front / Rear filter.
  • Oil change decals can show which car they're for: the plate, the last six of the VIN, or the year and model.

October 26, 2026

  • Put the customer portal on your website. A bar across the top, a button in the corner, or a menu link, in your brand colours. Paste one line of code once; change the wording or colours in RO Engine and your site follows. Preview it on your real site first. There's a QR code for the counter too.
  • Two brand colours for a two-colour look like black and yellow, with readable text on both.
  • The AI phone texts customers their portal link when they ask for an invoice copy or their history. Only to the number on their record, at most three a day.
  • Call transcripts read like a text conversation, with sentences that got talked over put back together.
  • Other part numbers on stock items. A part on your shelf is found under its OE number, the brand's number, or any other number you add.

October 23, 2026

  • The RO Engine Parts extension is on the Chrome Web Store.

October 22, 2026

  • Price check. Every part you buy again and again, priced at RockAuto, SSF, NAPA, O'Reilly and AutoZone from your own logins. Only the same part or a brand you'd fit counts toward the saving. Download it as a spreadsheet.
  • RockAuto prices in the Parts Catalog, beside your other suppliers.
  • Mnemos checks your shelf first before it looks at suppliers.
  • Recommend Services skips what's already on the RO, including the service the customer booked, and stops asking which engine a car has when the VIN already says.
  • Estimates start with only the urgent items ticked, so one tap no longer means yes to everything.
  • Estimates email to every customer with an address. Cash and check invoices show the cash discount as its own line, so the numbers add up.
  • See the customer's note ("call me with the price first") on their estimate answer.
  • Scan any part into inventory, not just fluids. Photograph the label and it finds the part you already stock or adds a new one.

October 21, 2026

  • Customer portal. Customers sign in with a code to their phone or email and see their cars, every invoice, and estimates waiting for an answer. Each car has its own page with every visit, the photos, and work still recommended. They can message you, see where their car is right now, and book. Off until you turn it on.
  • Send the invoice when you close it. Email it as a PDF with a link that opens on their phone, or text the link. No more printing.

October 20, 2026

  • Labor matrix. Set a multiplier by job size and a price by hours. It only touches estimated labor times; hours an advisor types are left alone. Estimated times over an hour round up to the next half hour, so 1.1 becomes 1.5. AutoHouse first
  • Taxable boxes on every service. Tick parts, labor, or both. Customers can be marked tax exempt. AutoHouse first
  • Closed means closed. An invoice can't close until every job is marked complete and the mileage out is in. A job that isn't getting done moves to recommendations instead of quietly dropping off. Once it's closed, every field is greyed out and an edit attempt tells you why. AutoHouse first
  • Clocking on a job makes it yours. If nobody has it, it's yours. If someone else has it, you're asked: take it and clock on, or just clock on. If it moves, whoever had it gets a push alert. AutoHouse first
  • Count stock with the fluid scanner. Scan a barcode, see what's on the shelf and what's spoken for on open ROs, tap in the count, scan the next one. Works with a handheld scanner too. AutoHouse first
  • Settings by role. Shop settings are Owner only; workflow settings are Manager and Owner. AutoHouse first

October 19, 2026

  • Mnemos drafts labor times with our own AI estimator. The advisor sets the final hours. AutoHouse first
  • Mnemos shops for parts when it writes a service: SSF, O'Reilly, AutoZone and NAPA, through the RO Engine Parts extension and your own logins. AutoHouse first
  • Change the vehicle on an RO. A misclick in the vehicle picker can be undone. An RO with no vehicle lists the customer's cars, or adds a new one. AutoHouse first
  • Move to Maintenance Recs or Repair Recs from any service's menu. AutoHouse first
  • RO numbers in the Communications inbox open that RO. AutoHouse first
  • Dates no longer jump to tomorrow in the evening. Each shop runs on its own time zone. AutoHouse first
  • The AI phone finds the caller's car without the year. AutoHouse first
  • "Take more photos" after sending check-in photos from a phone. AutoHouse first
  • Text consent from online booking is saved with the exact wording the customer agreed to. AutoHouse first

October 18, 2026

  • Maintenance list before the visit. When someone books, the shop can text a short list of what the car is due for, so the customer can mark what interests them before they arrive. Off until you turn it on.
  • Teach Mnemos your shop's rules. Type /teach and the rule. /rules lists them, /forget drops one.
  • Drivetrain on every vehicle. AWD, FWD or RWD narrows the maintenance schedule to the right one.
  • The AI phone says street names right. "Hwy" comes out as "Highway". New shops' assistant is named Milo.

October 17, 2026

  • No card to start. The 90-day trial starts with four fields: shop name, your name, email, shop link.
  • Launch price: $199 a month, locked for as long as you stay. Regular price $349.
  • Check-in list on the phone. Open check-in and see the cars due today, plus no-shows from the last 3 days, customer name first. Tap a car, shoot the VIN and odometer, hit Next car. AutoHouse first
  • Recalls, honestly. The recall badge no longer implies a model's recalls are open on this car. It sends you to NHTSA's VIN lookup and saves the answer on the vehicle. AutoHouse first

October 16, 2026

  • Call-back alerts. When the AI phone takes a call-back request, you get a dashboard card, a push alert, and a text to whoever you put on the list. AutoHouse first
  • We don't ask for your PartsTech password. Parts ordering runs through the RO Engine Parts browser extension, signed in with your own PartsTech login. AutoHouse first

October 15, 2026

  • Shop in PartsTech from the RO. PartsTech opens with the vehicle loaded. Your cart comes back onto the RO, and checkout is recorded as POs with confirmation numbers. AutoHouse first
  • Check in from any phone. Scan the QR code on the RO, shoot the VIN and odometer, and the desk fills in.
  • Special orders follow the job. Ordered parts are tied to their RO lines and show their status on the RO.
  • Inventory if you want it. Off by default. Parts come out of stock when the RO closes and go back if it's reopened or voided.
  • Fluids by the quart, whatever size the jug.
  • No automatic texts unless you turn them on.

October 14, 2026

  • Live job board on the dashboard. A lane for every tech. Drag a job to hand it over.
  • Shop-floor TV screen. The board on a TV in the shop.
  • Job clock. Techs clock on and off a job; time shows on My Hours.
  • Repair order tabs: My jobs, All open, Closed, Estimates.
  • Faster AI. Photo intake: about 13 seconds down to 2.5 for four photos. Fluid-bottle scanner: about 15 seconds down to 3–6. Vendor invoice scanning: 8.9 seconds down to 2.3.
  • QuickBooks for every shop, not just ours.
  • Security. Six rounds of fixes from a hands-on security audit of the live system.

October 8, 2026

  • Holiday closures and lunch breaks. Online booking and the AI phone both know when you're closed.
  • Inspections save as you go. No Save button. The camera stays open for the next photo.
  • New printed invoice.

October 7, 2026

  • The AI phone books from the same calendar as online booking, with the same rules. Your rules guide people; they're hard limits for the AI.
  • Waiter cap. Customers can't book past your daily waiter limit; staff can override.
  • Waiter / drop-off badge on every RO.

October 2, 2026

  • Hand-typed VINs get checked. Wrong length, illegal characters or a bad check digit warns you as you type. Year, make and model come from pick lists.

October 1, 2026

  • AI recommendations land on the RO as services you can edit like any other job.
  • One priority scale: critical, recommended, suggested. "Critical" now means well past due, not just due.

September 2026

  • Self-serve signup opened. Your own shop at yourshop.roengine.com, set up by a wizard built around writing your first RO.
  • Maintenance from the owner's manual. Recommendations use the manufacturer's printed intervals and fluid specs, with the page cited.
  • Texting through RO Engine, with carrier registration handled per shop.
  • Appointment confirmations and day-before reminders on every booking.
  • Online booking menu editor in Settings.
  • Label printing, USB or network, with your shop's own decal design.
  • Free Shop-Ware import for customers, vehicles and repair orders.
