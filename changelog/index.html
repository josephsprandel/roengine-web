RO Engine

Release notes

What changed in RO Engine, dated. Tell me what's broken and it shows up here. — Joe

Current release: 1.0.0 · build d72665d1 · November 5, 2026
Changes run at AutoHouse first. Anything tagged AutoHouse first reaches every shop with the next release.

November 11, 2026

  • Mnemos sorts out mixed-up POs and invoices. A PO that doesn’t match its invoice? Click Ask Mnemos on the PO, in Receiving’s Invoice vs PO, or on an invoice card. Mnemos compares them, says what’s off and offers the fix: merge a part listed twice, move an invoice to the right PO, settle a price. You approve each change first. AutoHouse first
  • Every scanned invoice in one place. Parts Manager has an Invoices tab: every vendor invoice that’s been scanned, newest first, with what was read off it and an Open button for the scan. Search by vendor, invoice #, PO, RO, or any part number or description on it. Each card says where it went: the PO it was received on, still waiting to be received, or set aside. AutoHouse first
  • Fix a part that came in twice on a PO. When a vendor bills a part under its own item number, receiving could add it as a second line, so a PO for 4 tires read 8. Open the PO, click Duplicate on the extra line and pick the line it duplicates. They become one line, with the invoice’s price and the vendor’s number kept, and the shelf count is put right. Works on received and reconciled POs too. Receiving now also spots the maker’s number in the invoice line’s description, so this happens less. AutoHouse first
  • Total up recommendations. Walking a customer through their options? Tick the box on each recommendation and a totals card opens below them: parts, labor, shop supplies, sales tax and the total, plus what the whole RO comes to with them. Untick them all and it goes away. Ticking doesn’t approve or save anything. AutoHouse first
  • Put checklist steps in order. In the canned job editor, drag a checklist step by its grip to move it. Put the multipoint in the order your techs walk the car, and every new RO lists the steps that way, on the RO and in the tech app. AutoHouse first
  • Call-backs: I’m on it, and fewer repeat desk calls. Seen a call-back and calling them yourself? Click I’m on it on the Call-backs card, and the phone assistant won’t ring the desk about it. It stays on the card until you click Handled. The assistant also stops calling about callers it already put through to someone, and when the desk says it’s already handled, that caller’s other call-backs are cleared too. AutoHouse first

November 10, 2026

  • Recommend services knows more of the car’s history. Recommend services times each item from the work this shop has done on the car, and it now reads far more of it: brake jobs, A/C services, transmission drain-and-fills, alignments, plugs and batteries filed as repairs, and every line of the older system’s invoices. Fewer “due” items the car just had done. Mnemos can answer “when was the brake fluid last done?” from the same records.
  • Added recommendations stay on the list. Add to RO now puts a new job in Services and leaves the recommendation where it was, still Approved, with an Added to RO badge. Click the badge to jump to the job. The recommendation keeps the price the customer approved. Added one by mistake? On the job, More actions → Move to Recs puts it back.
  • Recommend services is for advisors. Technicians no longer see Recommend services on the RO or in Actions. It builds the priced maintenance list for the customer, which is the advisor's job. A tech can still add a recommendation by hand with + Add recommendation.
  • Set when a promise reads TIGHT. Settings → Job Board → Promised-time warning: choose how much of the shop's open time before a promise should be left to spare before an RO shows TIGHT (25% to start).
  • When PartsTech is down. If Submit to PartsTech can't reach PartsTech, Order Parts now says nothing was ordered and points you to Phone or counter order — make the PO, right under it: call the supplier and give the counter that PO number.
  • A warning when a promised time is getting tight. Next to an RO's promised time — on the RO list, the RO itself and the job board — a TIGHT or WON'T MAKE IT chip shows when the work left on the jobs (booked hours, less the time already on the job clock) is close to or more than the shop's open hours before the promise, or a part isn't in yet. Hover it for the numbers. Nothing shows when the RO is on track.
  • Ask for a credit when the invoice price is higher. When you reconcile a purchase order against the vendor's invoice and a part was billed above the PO price, you can now pick Ask for credit: the PO keeps the price you were quoted, and Parts Manager → Returns follows the credit you're owed until the credit memo comes.
  • A part is never ordered twice. If two people have Order Parts open on the same RO, the second order leaves out any part that's already on a purchase order and says which PO has it. If everything is already ordered, nothing is sent.
  • Return parts to the vendor. On a purchase order, a received part has a Return button: how many, why, and the vendor's RA #. Parts Manager → Returns (where Cores lives now) follows each one from To return to Returned to Credited, with the credit you're owed. Keep inventory? A special order you didn't use can go onto the shelf instead.

November 9, 2026

  • Trend lines skip the month in progress. A line chart by month no longer dips at the end because the month has only just started: the month so far (and a first month the range starts in the middle of) is left off the line, with a note. The table still has it.
  • Package price and price per quart. Parts Manager shows what you pay for the package and, under it, what one quart, gallon or pound comes to: a $2,090 drum of oil sold by the quart shows $9.50/qt. Commodities count each fluid in its own unit too.
  • Quarts and gallons on the invoice. A fluid drawn from stock shows its unit on the RO, the customer's estimate and the invoice: "6.5 qt × $9.50/qt", "1 gal × $38.00/gal". The line keeps the unit it was added in, so changing how a part is counted later doesn't change jobs already written.
  • Each fluid in its own unit. Settings → Inventory → Sold as: engine oil by the quart, coolant by the gallon, refrigerant by the pound, one setting per kind of fluid. New items and label scans follow it. Receiving converts the package (a 55 gal drum adds 220 qt, a 30 lb cylinder adds 30 lb) and prices it per unit.
  • Parts Manager → Commodities. The parts and fluids you go through most, built from your own invoices: everything sold on more than 6 ROs in a year, with how many ROs, how much, what's on the shelf and a 12-month trend line. Keep or ignore each one, merge two numbers that are the same thing. It's the start of the inventory forecast.
  • Charts with one line per part. Ask Mnemos for a chart broken out by part number (or by tech, or by make) and it draws a separate line for each, with a legend. Reports can also look back 2, 3, 4 or 5 years now.
  • Report: Parts we sell often. Every part sold on more than 6 ROs in the last 12 months, with how many ROs, the quantity, ROs per month and the last 3 months, so you can see what's picking up and what's slowing down. It's the start of the inventory forecast.
  • The invoice names the tech who did the work. Closing an invoice hands the RO from whoever has it at the desk to the tech whose jobs carry the most hours, and that's the Technician on the invoice, in Tech hours and in the reports by technician. Only people with the new Does repair work permission (Technician and Owner by default) are credited with jobs.
  • See the parts invoice from the RO. Actions → Parts invoices shows the vendor's invoice for the RO's parts, the paper scanned or photographed when the parts came in. Works on closed ROs too.
  • Every approval is on the RO's Activity, the customer's and the advisor's, by service name, plus the invoice being closed, reopened or voided (with the reason), payments, jobs completed, discount changes and parts received. A price, quantity or labor time that changes on work the customer already approved is recorded too, before and after.
  • Scan invoices at the counter. Put a stack of vendor invoices in the scanner and press its button. ROe Print on that PC sends each scan to RO Engine, where it's read, matched to its PO, and waits in Receiving → Scanned invoices for you to check and receive. Works with any scanner that saves PDFs to a folder. AutoHouse first
  • Sort the Hours manager tables. Click a column on the Timesheet, Tech pay or Payroll table to sort, again to reverse. Clock In and Clock Out sort by time of day.
  • Any canned job can have a checklist. A brake service or a battery replacement carries its check steps, and the tech gets them on the RO. Jobs brought over from ShopWare get their inspections back as a checklist.
  • Add a check-in photo after the job has started. Add photo on the RO's Intake Photos, labeled by what it shows. Checking a car in again no longer moves a job that's under way back to Checked In. AutoHouse first
  • Postpone an appointment without a new date. Customer wants to put it off but doesn't know when? Clear the Arrival time on the RO. It comes off the Schedule, the RO and its estimate stay under Estimates, and the RO's Activity notes when it was booked for. Cleared it by mistake? Undo puts it right back. Set a new Arrival when they call back. AutoHouse first

  • Every-RO jobs on online and phone bookings. The jobs a shop adds to every RO, like a courtesy multipoint inspection, now go on ROs booked online and by the phone assistant too, the same as ones made at the counter. AutoHouse first
  • Mark a conversation as read. In Communications, a customer's text that was handled another way, like a call back, can be marked read: it stops showing as waiting on the shop but stays in the inbox, and the customer's next message makes it waiting again. AutoHouse first
  • RO Financials. A $ next to every RO's total turns green, yellow or red by how much the job makes, against margin targets each shop sets. Click it for the gross margin, profit per billed hour, what labor, parts and sublet sold for and cost, and a warning when a part has no cost entered. Owners can include what the techs are paid. AutoHouse first
